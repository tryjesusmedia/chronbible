Is ChronBible really free?

Yes! The complete online ChronBible is free to access. No purchase required.

Do I need to know much about the Bible?

Not at all. ChronBible is designed for beginners and experienced Bible readers alike.

Do I have to read every day?

No. You can read at your own pace and return whenever you're ready.

Does ChronBible change the Bible?

No. The ChronBible presents Scripture in a historical reading sequence. The biblical text remains unchanged. Some historical dates and the order of certain events are uncertain.

You can follow the ChronBible readings alongside your preferred Bible translation.

Can I use it on my phone or computer?

Yes! The online ChronBible works on compatible phones, tablets, and computers.
